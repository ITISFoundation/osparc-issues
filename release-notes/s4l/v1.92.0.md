## ✨ Highlights

### 🧍More ViP Models Available in The Shop

More Virtual Population (ViP) models are now available directly from **The Shop**.

The Shop is where you can rent **ViP models**, **ViZoo models**, and **Phantoms**, and then use them in your **Sim4Life simulations**. With this update, the catalog has grown with additional posable ViP models, giving you more anatomical options for your simulation workflows.

Newly available models include:

- **Yoon-sun** Posable V4.1
- **Jeduk** Posable V4.1
- **Duke** Posable V3.2
- **Ella** Posable V3.2
- **Fats** Posable V3.3
- **Billie** Posable V3.2
- **Thelonious** Posable V3.2
- **Yoon-sun** Posable V3.2

And as a friendly reminder: **Duke** and **Yoon-sun** are freely available to use, making them a great starting point if you want to explore ViP-based simulations in Sim4Life.

More models, more poses, more simulation possibilities.
