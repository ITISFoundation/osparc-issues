# Release Notes
### Version 1.98.0 · October 27, 2026

This release focuses on account security and platform stability: passwords are now protected with a stronger hashing algorithm, signing in reveals less about your account, and a set of fixes keeps studies running smoothly when multiple people (or services) work on them at the same time.

---

## ✨ Highlights

### 🎨 Preview Languages Labeled "Beta"

User interfaces available in a language that is still in preview are now clearly marked as **beta**, so you always know when a translation may still be work in progress ([#9770](https://github.com/ITISFoundation/osparc-simcore/pull/9770)).

---

### 🔒 Stronger Password Protection

Passwords are now hashed with **Argon2id**, the current state-of-the-art password hashing algorithm ([#9809](https://github.com/ITISFoundation/osparc-simcore/pull/9809)).

There is nothing to do on your side: accounts keep working as before, and stored passwords are upgraded automatically as you sign in.

In the same spirit, the login page **no longer reveals whether an email is registered** on the platform ([#9826](https://github.com/ITISFoundation/osparc-simcore/pull/9826)) — making it harder for third parties to probe which accounts exist.

---

### 🐛 A More Reliable Platform

You may not notice these changes — and that's the point. We fixed several situations that could occasionally make a study feel stuck or unresponsive, especially when several people work on it at the same time ([#9818](https://github.com/ITISFoundation/osparc-simcore/pull/9818), [#9827](https://github.com/ITISFoundation/osparc-simcore/pull/9827), [#9830](https://github.com/ITISFoundation/osparc-simcore/pull/9830)).

Your analyses now keep running smoothly even when a storage service is temporarily slow ([#9816](https://github.com/ITISFoundation/osparc-simcore/pull/9816)), and cancelling a task that is waiting in line or interrupted by a short network hiccup behaves reliably again ([#9798](https://github.com/ITISFoundation/osparc-simcore/pull/9798), [#9799](https://github.com/ITISFoundation/osparc-simcore/pull/9799)).
